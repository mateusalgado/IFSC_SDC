Communication Systems Lab 02/09 · Dipole and patch antennas

Antenna dimensions make more sense when expressed in wavelengths.

In this IFSC lab, I compared normalized dipole radiation patterns and calculated four rectangular patch designs. I examined how frequency, substrate permittivity, thickness and feed position affect the results.

The attached report brings together the radiation patterns, calculated dimensions and design comparisons.
