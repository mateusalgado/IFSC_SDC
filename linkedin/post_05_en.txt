Communication Systems Lab 05/09 · Coherent BFSK

Two carrier frequencies can represent one binary data stream.

In this IFSC lab, I studied BFSK modulation and a receiver with two correlators. Comparing the tones highlighted how spacing, phase consistency and signal energy affect detection.

The attached report includes the recorded results and explains why energy normalization and sample size must be checked before interpreting a BER curve.
